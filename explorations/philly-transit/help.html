What the transit modes mean

Bus

Road vehicles. Flexible routing, street traffic, and the biggest share of the live feed.

Trolley

Street-running rail. Think light rail or streetcars, especially the subway-surface trolley lines.

Subway / Metro

Rapid transit: Market-Frankford Line, Broad Street Line, Broad-Ridge Spur, and Norristown High Speed Line.

Regional Rail

Commuter rail. Longer-distance SEPTA trains with bigger station spacing and a separate operating pattern.

Why subway trains may be missing

NO GPS on SEPTA Metro is not a bug in this app. The Market-Frankford Line and Broad Street Line do not publish public GPS coordinates the way buses and trolleys do.

So when this map says a subway line is active but unlocated, it means trains are running, but SEPTA did not publish usable coordinates.

  • GPS live: direct coordinate from the feed
  • Stop inferred: no coordinate, but SEPTA reported a specific stop
  • Estimated: short projection from a recent usable position
  • Stale estimate: older projection kept only as a hint

What the map can and cannot know

Buses and trolleys generally behave like a standard vehicle GPS map. Metro does not. The app can show route activity and, in limited cases, stop-based placement or short extrapolation. It does not invent precise subway coordinates.

Replay is a client-side visualization built from the latest known report and route geometry. Useful, but not exact history.
